About the Advocate

Two Decades of
Integrity & Excellence

V. Narayanan is a distinguished Senior Advocate and Notary Public with over two decades of dedicated legal practice in Puducherry. With a reputation built on trust, precision, and unwavering commitment to justice, he has established himself as one of the most respected legal professionals in the region.

As a recognised Notary Public, he is empowered to authenticate and certify a wide range of legal documents — bringing the highest standard of legal authority to every matter he handles. His practice spans property documentation, affidavit attestation, legal consultation, and agreement drafting.

His approach combines deep legal expertise with a personal commitment to understanding each client's unique circumstances — ensuring outcomes that are both legally sound and genuinely in the best interests of those he serves.

"Legal counsel is not merely a service — it is a responsibility. Every client deserves clarity, honesty, and the full weight of my experience working in their favour."
